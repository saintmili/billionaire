# Settle replay intervals before boundary funding and price points

Accepted for offline M5 ticket #19 on 2026-11-17. A completed primary candle describes the interval before its exclusive end; replay settles that interval through the shared protection/liquidation services, then applies funding at the boundary before same-time close/open price points and new entries. Narrow isolated replay phases preserve this order at one timestamp, so a partial exit reduces funding exposure and a separately supplied funding mark can act on the stop ratcheted by the completed interval; a ratchet cannot act retrospectively within its own interval.

Timestamp sorting alone would either apply funding retroactively after accepted prices or advance protection state and silently skip the overlapping completed bar. Synthetic clock offsets depend on persistence precision, while broadly relaxing overlap checks would let future collateral or trailing state affect the past. Retain the shared financial owner and forward-paper strict-after rules from ADR 0002; the frozen manifest names the replay model, configuration retains liquidation parameters, and funding retains its declared expected schedule, exact events, marks and provenance.

Funding inside a primary OHLC interval, delayed funding, and supplied funding combined with recorded-close revision replay are rejected because available observations cannot causally segment those intervals. Use an aligned lower primary timeframe with finer observations; an empty or incomplete declared funding schedule remains unknown or missing coverage. This deliberately trades accepted datasets for causal, reproducible execution under ADRs 0003/0004; broader support requires explicit intrabar observations and a separately versioned model, rather than an optimistic invented path.
